# Privacy Policy — JStar Logistics

**Effective Date: 30 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

JStar Logistics is a business application designed for precast concrete production monitoring, delivery scheduling, transportation coordination and operational reporting.

The application is intended for authorised employees, transport companies and construction project customers.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and managed when using JStar Logistics.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the user's role and the features used, we may collect:

**Account Information**
- Usernames, names and email addresses.
- Authentication information and assigned user roles.
- User identifiers associated with registered accounts.

**Operational Information**
- Delivery schedules, delivery orders and invoice references.
- Project details, factory loading locations and transport company information.
- Vehicle registration numbers and departure records.
- Precast concrete production, delivery and stock information.
- User-entered remarks and operational updates.

**Uploaded Content**
- Delivery order photographs and related business documents uploaded through the application.

**Technical Information**
- Device or installation identifiers used by enabled services.
- Application usage and diagnostic information, where collected by installed services.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

- Authenticate users and manage access permissions.
- Schedule, monitor and coordinate deliveries.
- Maintain delivery orders and operational records.
- Display production information and stock availability.
- Generate reports and performance summaries.
- Maintain application functionality, security and reliability.

## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers

JStar Logistics uses Google Firebase services, including authentication, cloud database storage and file storage.

Information is transmitted to and processed through these services to support application functionality.

Service providers are required to handle information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and their contractual obligations.

## 5. Access and Sharing

Application access is restricted to authorised users.

Information is made available according to assigned roles, including administrators, supervisors, transporters and project users.

Users may share delivery order documents through external applications when they choose to use the sharing functionality. Information shared externally may then be subject to the receiving service's privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Location Information

JStar Logistics does not use GPS to continuously monitor users or vehicles.

Delivery progress is recorded through operational updates submitted by authorised users.

## 7. Data Security

We use authentication, role-based access controls and appropriate technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support business operations, maintain required records and fulfil applicable legal obligations.

Users may contact the application administrator to request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information.

Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable legal, contractual and legitimate business record-retention requirements.

## 9. User Choices

Users may contact the application administrator to request information about their personal data, request corrections or seek account deactivation or deletion.

Some operational records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.

## 10. Children's Privacy

JStar Logistics is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data-processing practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through the application's published privacy policy webpage.
